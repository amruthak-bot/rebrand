002 · Round 3

What's new in Round 3

The three improvements we promised after Round 2, and exactly where to see each one. Everything below works in this live build.

01

See the damage before saving a rule change

Before a new guideline version is saved, ReBrand re-checks every live post against the proposed rules and shows which would newly fail, which rule causes it, and how many fix tasks that means. Nothing is saved until a person confirms.

Try it: Brand rules → change something → "Preview the impact first".

02

Claims that expire, with renewal and owner notices

Offers and claims have expiry dates. ReBrand scans on a timer, warns the owner of any live post using a claim that is running out, opens a fix task when one expires, and a renewal needs a manager's written evidence.

Try it: Plan ahead → "Free shipping on orders over $50" is expiring soon. Press "Check expiry and notify owners".

03

ReBrand learns from disputes

When writers keep disputing the same rule, ReBrand groups their reasons and drafts clearer wording. A manager or reviewer edits and approves it; only then does it become a new rules version. No wording changes itself.

Try it: Plan ahead → "Rules writers keep disputing".

04

Fixed since Round 2

Demo data no longer vanishes on redeploy (the app reseeds itself). Image posts are now read with OCR and checked like text. Sessions expire after 7 days. New here: one-click judge sign-ins, a demo story, and the Activity tab showing every approval and fix.

Suggested 3-minute path: New post → "Fill in a sample off-brand post" → Check. Then Review → approve. Then Live posts and Plan ahead.
